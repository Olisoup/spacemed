# Welcome to Olivier's BIO ¨\_(°v°)_/¨

## Humble beginnings
Born in Paris XVIIth arrondissement, I was raised speaking English thanks to my American mother (kudos to my dad for speaking english all the time despite his Frenchness). To my surprise and 
great disappointment, preschoolers in France don't speak English. This was one the first of many huddles to cross for little Olivier. At 6 years old we moved to Turkey for my dad's job, which 
once again, to my great disappointment, turkish 6 year olds don't speak turkish.

## Education
I went to preschool near Paris, then elementary school in Turkey, in Bursa. It was a 100% turkish school, which meant I had to learn turkish to get an education. At the same time I was taking 
correspondance classes for French, since we would be returning to France at some point (who on earth knows ?).

We returned to france in 2010, and I was starting middle school. Acne-ridden, brace wearing Olivier discovered the reality of French catholic schools, and the pressure associated with them. 8 to 
5 days, with lots of extra school work and exams. But overall I would say I had a good time. I fit the mold quite well. I had skipped a grade in Turkey so I was one of the "short kings". My main 
social mechanic to deal with bullying was initally unbridled rage (think of an angry leprechaun) and once I realised that didn't work it very quickly switched to humor. That seems to work a lot 
better.

From there it was a pretty standard experience. Feel deep into the Minecraft rabbithole which led to me becoming an all-around nerd. I really enjoyed school despite the hellish french system 
consisting of 8 to 5 days every day with 4 hour exams each week. At least after that finals seem reaaly easy I guess. Despite being good in school, I didn't get into any of my requested 
universities for engineering, due to changes in the uni application system. This led to big disappointment and in a sense, at the time made me edgy and think everything was unfair, blatantly 
unaware of my privilege in general. Once I took the time to think I decided to do better, aced finals an applied to medical school in the UK, since I spoke English it seemed like the obvious 
choice.

Those 6 years at Imperial College flew by, I learned a lot, but also discovered how little I actually know. It is a very humbling experience. I like to think that my main takeaway from 25 years 
of walking around on this rock is that

> Teamwork makes the dreamwork ! - Albert Einstein or something I don't know don't quote me

And after all that studying I realised once again that Admin staff don't reaaally care about the person behind the email. Brexit complicated many things for me, such as practising medicine in 
France. *WHOOOOPS* what a silly goose I am 🤡

I then applied to Space Medicine due to my passion for learning and honestly it just sounded really cool ! I really want to get involved with public health and climate research, because I 
sincerely believe that we have the opportunity right now to stop things before it's too late *hush some might say it already is though* but I want to be an optimist. I think being forward 
thinking and trying t prevent problems before we're affected is what will help us. Even though capitalistic greed is really trying to prevent us from doing that. This cohort really gives me 
hope.
